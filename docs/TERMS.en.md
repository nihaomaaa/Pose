Version: 2026-10-29.1

## Noncommercial use

Pose is source-available software for noncommercial learning, personal research and sharing. Do not sell the app, sell access to it, provide paid generation services or use it for commercial profit. Original Pose code is licensed under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. Third-party components, models and the gallery retain their separate terms.

## Your images and permissions

Use only images you own or have permission to process, including the depicted people’s consent. Respect copyright, likeness, reputation and privacy rights. “Learning only” does not excuse infringement. Do not create nonconsensual intimate imagery, sexual content involving minors, fraud, impersonation, defamation or other unlawful content.

## Local and cloud processing

Local generation keeps photos and outputs in your chosen data folder. Setup contacts runtime, model and gallery download servers, which receive normal network information such as your IP address. Cloud generation sends the prompt and required images to the provider you explicitly configure, potentially outside your country. Review its privacy, retention and training policies before use.

## Billing, moderation and results

API charges are billed by the provider; a website subscription may not cover API usage. Moderation can reject a request, and stopping a task may not stop provider billing. AI output can change identity, clothing, anatomy and details. Inspect every result before use. Pose does not promise identity accuracy or bypass safety controls.

## AI disclosure

Outputs are AI-generated, not evidence of real events. When publishing them, disclose AI generation and use the destination platform’s labeling tools as required by applicable law. Do not maliciously remove or falsify provider watermarks or provenance labels.

## Storage and deletion

Keys are encrypted with Windows user protection. Uploads, outputs and logs stay in the local data folder until you remove them. Deleting an artwork does not delete its source photo or a provider’s copies. Uninstalling preserves the data folder. Never share keys or private data in GitHub issues.
